We put cameras on home cages, record around the clock, and measure behavior from every
frame. Continuous recording catches what short assays miss: in developing gerbils it
revealed structured social behavior well before the animals become independent
([Mitelut et al., PLoS Biology 2025](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3003348)).

The same pipeline is being used to look for early signs of neurodegeneration in mouse
models, and to study animals in spaceflight
([Duporge et al., npj Microgravity 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41526-025-00460-5)).
