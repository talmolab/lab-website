We develop tools to quantify traits in **complex morphologies** such as plant root
systems. We are applying these technologies to
[solve climate change](https://www.salk.edu/science/power-of-plants/) in collaboration
with experimental labs in the
[Salk Harnessing Plants Initiative](https://www.salk.edu/harnessing-plants-initiative/).
