Elizabeth is a software engineer who joined the lab in January 2024. She received her B.S.
in Physics and Biochemistry from Chapman University and M.S. in Physics from the University
of Chicago. She first worked jointly with Wolfgang Busch on computer vision-based approaches
for phenotyping plant root system architectures, including the development of SLEAP and
cloud-based platforms for data integration. Since October 2026 she has worked jointly with
Eiman Azim on the lab's Virtual Neuroscience Lab.
