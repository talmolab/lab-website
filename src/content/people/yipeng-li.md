Yipeng "Leo" Li is a PhD student in the UC San Diego Neurosciences Graduate Program,
co-advised by Kuo-Fen Lee at Salk. He joined the lab in September 2025 as a research
assistant. He received his B.S. in Mathematics and B.S. in Human Biology from UCSD, and his
