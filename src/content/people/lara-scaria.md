Lara Scaria is a PhD student in the UC San Diego Neurosciences Graduate Program, co-advised
by Eiman Azim, who joined the lab in July 2026. Lara received a B.S. in Neuroscience and
Computer Science from Brandeis University, worked as a research assistant in Eve Marder's lab
at Brandeis, and was then a research associate in Rui Costa's group at the Allen Institute.
In the lab, Lara uses AI-based behavioral tracking to study ALS in mouse models.
