Grace Niyo is a postdoctoral fellow who joined the lab in August 2026, jointly with the Azim
lab. Grace received a B.S. in Biomedical Engineering from the University of Rochester, then
M.S. degrees in Biomedical Engineering and Electrical Engineering and a Ph.D. in Biomedical
Engineering from the University of Southern California, advised by Francisco Valero-Cuevas.
Grace's doctoral work used computational neuromechanics to study how spinal circuits, in
particular the fusimotor system, contribute to the control of voluntary movement, with
first-author results published in PNAS. In the lab, Grace works on the Virtual Neuroscience
Lab.
