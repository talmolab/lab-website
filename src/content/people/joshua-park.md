Joshua is a graduate student at UC San Diego. He joined the lab in December 2024 as an
undergraduate double majoring in Computer Engineering and Neurobiology at UC San Diego, and
is broadly interested in computational neuroscience. Joshua was a 2023 [UCSD URS Eureka!](https://ugresearch.ucsd.edu/research-programs/eureka/index.html) Summer Fellow in the Chalasani Lab, where he developed an automated pipeline for analyzing brain activity patterns. His work focuses on 3D pose estimation, including the LUC3D project, and on building virtual animals to do virtual neuroscience using embodied neuromechanical simulations.
